# Crochet, Nails & Jewelry Keyword Research

Research date: September 1, 2026. Market: English-language, U.S.-first editorial site with Pinterest as the primary visual discovery channel.

## Method and limits

The launch map combines live Google/Pinterest result patterns, Pinterest related-interest language, Pinterest Predicts 2026, and the site's ability to satisfy the query with an original visual guide. No search-volume, keyword-difficulty or CPC numbers are invented. Connect Google Search Console and a licensed keyword dataset after launch to replace qualitative assumptions with first-party impressions and validated third-party metrics.

Selection rules:

1. A clear reader task can be completed on the page.
2. The query has a strong visual/save intent suitable for Pinterest.
3. Each page owns one primary intent to reduce cannibalization.
4. Trend pages are paired with evergreen support pages.
5. Health, material and craft-standard claims cite authoritative sources.

## Cannibalization boundaries

- `easy crochet flowers` owns the single-motif beginner task; `crochet flower bouquet` owns multi-stem composition; `crochet flower bag` owns a lined usable bag.
- `short almond nails` owns shape-specific inspiration; `nail shapes guide` owns shape selection; `cat-eye chrome` and `lace nails` own distinct finishes.
- `how to layer necklaces` owns necklace spacing; `ring stacking guide` owns hand placement; `jewelry metals guide` owns material definitions; `birthstone jewelry guide` owns month/gift intent.

## Research evidence

- Pinterest’s official 2026 report identifies **Laced Up** as a trend: <https://business.pinterest.com/pinterest-predicts/2026/laced-up/>
- Pinterest’s related-interest pages surfaced exact language around easy crochet flowers, cat-eye chrome, short almond nails, necklace layering and ring stacking.
- Google Search Central recommends descriptive, crawlable internal links and people-first content: <https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable> and <https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content>
- Craft facts reference Craft Yarn Council standards: <https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards>
- Nail product guidance references FDA and AAD: <https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetic-products/nail-care-products> and <https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/nail-care-secrets>
- Jewelry material and care guidance references FTC and GIA: <https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/buying-platinum-gold-and-silver-jewelry> and <https://www.gia.edu/gia-news-research-tips-caring-jewelry>

## Post-launch validation

At 14, 30 and 60 days, export Search Console query/page data and Pinterest outbound-click/save data. Promote pages with impressions but low CTR by testing title and Pin framing. Expand pages that earn saves and long-tail impressions; do not create near-duplicate pages until the query data shows a distinct intent.
