# The Curated Pin editorial focus

## Primary promise

The Curated Pin helps readers plan joyful birthdays and finish useful handmade
projects. New content must serve a clear planning or making task rather than
expanding into an unrelated visual niche simply because it performs on Pinterest.

## Active authority clusters

### Birthday Parties — proven traffic engine

- Ideas by age, including first, tween, teen and milestone birthdays
- Party themes with coordinated activities, food and decorations
- Games organized by group size, age, setting and energy level
- Practical food, setup, budget and timeline guidance
- Printables, planners and reusable checklists

Every existing Birthday URL remains unchanged.

### Crochet/Crafts — active expansion

- Beginner techniques and learning sequences
- Flowers, bouquets and handmade decor
- Bags, blankets and finishable projects
- Yarn, hook, sizing and gauge guidance
- Handmade gifts and celebration-adjacent projects

## Complementary content filter

A future topic belongs on The Curated Pin only when it passes all four checks:

1. The Birthday or Crochet audience would reasonably use it.
2. It solves a specific planning, making or buying decision.
3. It can link naturally to at least two existing pages in the same cluster.
4. The site can add original instructions, a template, a test or a useful tool.

Best next expansions are DIY birthday decorations, printable party games,
handmade favors, gift wrapping, crochet gifts and seasonal celebration crafts.

Jewelry, Nails and Beauty belong to Aurora. Wedding templates and planning
belong to Wedding Gazette. Cats and Dogs belong to the future pet publication.
