# Ethical Backlink Launch Plan

Backlinks cannot be installed inside this repository. They are earned when an independent publisher chooses to cite a useful page. Buying links, automated link creation and excessive exchanges risk violating Google’s spam policies.

## Prospect tiers

| Tier | Prospect type | Pitch angle |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Craft educators, libraries, yarn shops | Cite or share the stitch order and gauge estimator as classroom resources |
| 1 | Nail educators, salons, beauty newsletters | Use the structure-first shape chart or safety checklist as a client resource |
| 1 | Jewelers, consumer publishers, fashion educators | Reference FTC-aligned metal definitions and the necklace/ring diagrams |
| 2 | Lifestyle newsletters and gift publishers | Include the bouquet, birthstone or wearable nail visual in a seasonal roundup |
| 2 | Pinterest creators with owned sites | Collaborate on a visual citation with a canonical link to the full guide |
| 3 | Resource pages and relevant associations | Suggest the most specific utility asset, never a generic homepage link |

## Quality controls

- Personalize every email to a specific existing page.
- Pitch one relevant asset, not all 15 articles.
- Do not pay for followed editorial links.
- Mark sponsorships appropriately when a commercial relationship exists.
- Track contacted, replied, cited and declined separately.
- Stop follow-up after two respectful attempts.
- Earned links should point to the page that contains the cited asset.
