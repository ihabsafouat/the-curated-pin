## Publishing checklist

1. In Gumroad, choose **New product → Digital product**.
2. Paste the appropriate title, price, short summary, full description, and tags from above.
3. Upload the matching cover image and the correct customer PDF file.
4. Check the purchase page in a private browser window before publishing.
5. For each paid product, copy its public Gumroad product URL after publishing.
6. In Netlify, add that URL as the matching `CHECKOUT_URL_...` environment variable.
7. Trigger a new production deployment and test both purchase buttons.
8. If listing the free products on Gumroad, also keep the website's direct free download available; Gumroad is optional for those files.
