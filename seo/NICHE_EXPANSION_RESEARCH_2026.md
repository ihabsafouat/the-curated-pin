# Niche Expansion Research — September 2026

## Decision

1. **Skincare and makeup: test next inside Style.** Current public U.S. Pinterest
   shopping trends show Serums & Essences outbound clicks up 52% month over month.
   The category has strong product-comparison, routine and ingredient-order click gaps.
2. **Cats and dogs: attractive market, separate publication recommended.** The U.S.
   pet market reached $158 billion in 2025 and is projected at $165 billion for 2026,
   but veterinary and health-adjacent content requires specialist sourcing and review.
3. **Do not create several niche accounts pointing to one claimed domain.** Pinterest
   states that a website can be claimed by only one Pinterest account. Use one account
   per owned domain or one umbrella account with niche boards.

## Weighted opportunity model

| Niche | Outbound-click potential | Monetization | Fit with The Curated Pin | Risk / cost | Recommendation |

## Click-first Skincare / Makeup pilot

- Skincare routine order by skin type
- Serum layering chart: compatible combinations and irritation cautions
- How to patch-test a skincare product
- Makeup brush cleaning schedule and method
- Makeup for glasses: placement and transfer control
- Foundation undertone decision guide

Use American Academy of Dermatology and FDA sources. Avoid diagnosing conditions,
promising treatment, or calling cosmetics FDA-approved. Product rankings require
firsthand testing and documented comparison criteria.

## Separate Pets publication: first clusters

### Dogs

- New puppy schedule and supply checklist
- Dog enrichment ideas by space and energy level
- Dog travel packing checklist
- Grooming-tool comparison based on coat type
- Questions to ask a veterinarian at a new-pet visit

### Cats

- New cat safe-room checklist
- Indoor-cat enrichment rotation
- Cat carrier sizing and acclimation guide
- Multi-cat resource placement plan
- Senior-cat observation checklist with clear veterinarian escalation language

Do not publish treatment, medication, diet-change or emergency instructions without
qualified veterinary review. Link urgent concerns to a veterinarian or emergency clinic.
