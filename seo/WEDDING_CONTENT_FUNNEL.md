# Wedding Content Funnel

## Commercial boundary

The Wedding Gazette landing page owns the transactional offer: editable wedding
newspapers, signage, reception games, favors and the $9–$59 product ladder. Blog
articles own informational queries and send qualified readers to that offer.

| Blog page | Primary keyword | Funnel role | Product connection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wedding Newspaper Ideas | wedding newspaper ideas | Inspiration + content planning | Lite, Essential and Complete Gazette |
| Wedding Welcome Sign Wording and Sizes | wedding welcome sign wording | Wording + production decision | 43-piece signage collection |
| Wedding Reception Games for Guests | wedding reception games for guests | Activity planning | 15 reception activities |
| Wedding Stationery Checklist | wedding stationery checklist | Full-system planning | Complete Suite and Ultimate Vault |
| Edit and Print a Wedding Newspaper | how to make a wedding newspaper | Template use + print readiness | Lite and Essential Gazette |

The commercial phrase `wedding newspaper template` should remain primarily owned
by the product page. Informational articles may discuss the template but should not
duplicate the product page's title, pricing comparison or full offer copy.

## Conversion rules

- Use one contextual product block after the article has answered the first major question.
- Link to the Wedding Gazette landing page, not straight to checkout, so readers can compare editions.
- Preserve product facts: 60+ editable templates, A4 and US formats, Canva + PowerPoint workflow, instant digital delivery.
- Do not invent reviews, scarcity, discounts or customer results.
- Measure article-to-product clicks separately from affiliate clicks.

## Pinterest creative handoff

Wedding Pin graphics are intentionally not bundled in this release. Build them after
reviewing the user's proven wedding Pins. Each article should receive at least three
creative angles: emotional outcome, practical problem and bold checklist/guide promise.
